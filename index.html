How the plans are worked out

Roads can go on any land tile. Mountains and water block them. City tiles already count as roads, so passing through a city costs nothing.

Fewest roads is a minimum Steiner tree, solved exactly (Dreyfus–Wagner) so no smaller network exists. Exact solving works for up to 12 cities on maps up to Large size, and fewer on bigger maps. Past that limit it uses a fast approximation.

Fastest travel gives every pair of cities its shortest land route, overlapping routes wherever it can, then removes any tile that doesn't shorten a trip.

Balanced starts from the fewest-roads network and adds the shortcut with the best travel saved per new tile, again and again, until no shortcut beats the slider. A shortcut only counts if it makes at least one trip 2 or more tiles shorter. Shortcuts that save a single tile are never built.

Total travel adds up the trip length for every pair of cities. Upkeep assumes 1 gold per road tile per turn.
